ArthFlow

Take control of your money

ArthFlow is a personal finance management solution designed to help users organize income, track expenses, and manage their financial activities with maximum privacy and offline availability.

Development Status & Transparency

ArthFlow is currently in active development and internal alpha testing. Features described below reflect currently implemented core functionality and prioritized release milestones.

Current & Core Features:
Income Tracking

Easily log multiple income sources, salary streams, and freelance payments with date records.

Expense Management

Record and tag everyday outflows into standard spending buckets (utilities, food, tech, transport).

Financial Overview

Instant visual summary of net cash flow, balance trends, and category distribution.

Transaction Organization

Filter and search transactions by date ranges, payment modes, and custom labels.

Personal Finance Insights

Generate monthly comparisons and identify budget leakage areas without third-party exposure.
